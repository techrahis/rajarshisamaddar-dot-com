## Step 5: Making Professional Connections and Networking

The ability to network is crucial for landing internships. Expand your professional network by attending conferences, employment fairs, and industry events. Talk to people in the industry, introduce yourself, and say that you are interested in internships.

The network of recent graduates from your university might be a useful source because they have connections and knowledge about internship opportunities. Also, request informational interviews from experts on LinkedIn or via cold email to find out more about their careers and get helpful guidance.

Developing meaningful connections with experts in the subject can lead to internship possibilities that aren’t always posted on job portals. Numerous large product-based firms, like Microsoft, Amazon, Adobe, Facebook, and Netflix, have a referral strategy whereby their employees are given a certain number of recommendations each year so they can recommend people from their networks who could possess the necessary skill set. But keep in mind that in order to receive a referral, you must first establish a solid rapport; although daunting at first, this is not the case.

## Step 7: Acing the Interview

Congratulations! You have been selected to attend an interview. It’s time to be ready for the interview and ace it now. Please conduct comprehensive background research on the company before you appear in the interview. Look at its services or products, culture, and most recent successes.

Prepare for the commonly asked interview questions that highlight your technical expertise, capacity for problem-solving, and prior experience in areas related to the internship. Practice giving clear, to-the-point answers to questions while emphasizing your passion for computer science and engineering (CSE) and your ability to contribute to the company’s goal.

Since you come from a technological background, the first round of the interview will be technical, with questions relating to fundamental computer science concepts to the internship you have applied for. Some businesses may have simple aptitude tests on their list.

Prepare a few relevant questions about the firm, team, or internship program to ask the interviewer as well. Typically, the interviewer will ask you if you have any questions to them, so be ready for that as well. Whether the interview is conducted offline or online, you should look professional and present yourself with humility and confidence. Learn the fundamentals of body language and adhere to them.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Can I apply for internships outside of India?

Absolutely! Many companies offer internships to international students. However, it’s important to go through the requirements and qualifying standards listed by the company.

### Are internships paid for CSE students?

Both sorts of internships are available on the market; one is paid, another is unpaid. However, I strongly advise against taking any paid internships.

### Can earn money from internships?

Yes, internships do provide money as a stipend, but they also come with intimidating screening processes that you must pass through.

### How long do internships typically last?

The duration of internships can vary depending on the company and the specific position. Internships can range from a few weeks to several months, some companies even offer year-long internships.

### Can I intern at multiple companies simultaneously?

Although it is technically possible to intern at more than one company at once, juggling the workload might be difficult. It’s best to concentrate on one internship at a time so that you can give it all of your attention and effort and get the most out of it.
